Each card shows the icon at home-screen size, small sizes, and on light, dark and busy wallpapers. Use the sliders to tweak hue, saturation and brightness live, then tell me the number and the values.

What the research says

  • One bold symbol, 1 to 2 focal points. Thin lines, small text and busy textures vanish at small sizes. Check at about 60 px.
  • Restrained gradients, flat foreground. iOS 26/27 Liquid Glass adds its own light, refraction and edge highlights, so don't bake in bevels, glass or heavy shadows.
  • Strong symbol-versus-background contrast. iOS 27 pushes the symbol further forward from the background.
  • Rounded forms. Light wraps rounded corners better than sharp ones.
  • Stand out from blue. Blue is the most common choice, so most navigation and car apps blend together.
  • Design for all appearances. Default, dark, clear and tinted; tinted wants a white symbol on dark. Thin or low-contrast details disappear there.
  • Avoid unfortunate resemblances. Soft stacked blobs can read as something else; hard geometry avoids it.
